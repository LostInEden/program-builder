# CounterScheme — Questions for Coach

*From Matt. Answer as many as you can, in any order, however is easiest — typed, voice memos, photos of a whiteboard, or talking it through on a call. Partial answers help. "I don't know yet" is a real answer. Skip anything that doesn't apply.*

*Why this matters: the app only knows what you tell it. Every answer below turns into a rule, a screen, or a default we stop guessing at.*

---

## A. How you actually work

1. Walk us through your week from Saturday morning to Friday kickoff. When do you watch film, when do you scout, when do you build the game plan, and when is it "locked"?
2. Which days and times would you realistically open this app? (Sunday night? Every morning? Only Tuesday and Wednesday?)
3. Who else touches the defense besides you — coordinator, position coaches, a GA? Should any of them be able to *change* things in the app, or only see them?
4. Where do you do this work — laptop, iPad, phone, a tablet in the press box? Which one most?
5. What do you use today for each job: Hudl (which plan — do you have Hudl Assist / auto-breakdowns?), MaxPreps, Excel or Google Sheets, paper? What's the one thing you'd be happiest to stop doing by hand?
6. Team identity for the app: school name, mascot, colors, your name and title, season year, level (Varsity). Right now it says "Demo High School / Coach Linville" as a placeholder.

## B. My Team

7. Can you send a real roster export (Hudl, MaxPreps, or SportsEngine — a CSV or spreadsheet)? Blank out names if you want. We build the importer against whatever columns your file actually has.
8. What do you call your defensive positions — your words? (Mike, Will, Sam, Jack, Rover, Nickel, Star, Field/Boundary corner, etc.) Which body type plays each?
9. Depth chart: how deep do you keep it — 2 or 3 per spot? Do you keep separate depth charts for Base, Nickel, Dime, Goal Line, etc., or one chart with sub-packages? Which packages do you actually carry in a season?
10. Player skill ratings: we built five — Tackling, Coverage, Block Shedding, Pursuit/Effort, Football IQ — each 1 to 5. Are those the right five? Anything to add (speed, physicality, ball skills, run-fit discipline) or drop? Would you grade a lineman on different things than a corner?
11. Does 1–5 feel right, or do you think in letter grades, or "starter / backup / developmental"?
12. Which measurables do you actually test, and how often? (40, pro shuttle, vertical, broad, bench, squat, clean, flying 10.) Can you send a weight-room sheet from last testing?
13. Injuries: is Healthy / Limited / Out enough, or do you want an expected return date and a note?
14. Watch List: what is that list for in your head — kids close to starting? Attitude? Grades and eligibility? Something else?
15. The roster shows an overall star rating per player. Useful, or noise?

## C. My Scheme (the most important section)

16. Send us your defense as it exists today, in whatever form: install book, call sheet, wristband, playbook PDF, front/coverage/blitz lists, a photo of the whiteboard. Anything.
17. What is your base defense, and how many DL / LB / DB? (The app currently defaults to 3-4; your mock showed 4-2-5 Under.)
18. List your fronts by name (Over, Under, Tite, Bear, Mint, Okie…). Which is base? Which do you actually call most?
19. List your coverages by name and which is base. We loaded your 30-coverage Match Coverage Library — for the ones you run, do those rules match how you teach it? What would you change or say differently?
20. List your pressures/blitzes by name. How do you group them (zone, man, edge, 3rd-down calls, packages)? What coverage sits behind each one?
21. Give us 10–20 real adjustments in this exact shape: **"When ___, we ___, so the call is ___."** Cover: vs formations (trips, empty, bunch, unbalanced), vs motion (jet, across, orbit), vs personnel (12, 13, 21, 22, heavy), situational (3rd & long, 2-minute), special (red zone, goal line, 4th down, 2-point).
22. Your terminology, as a word list: what you call formations (Trips, Trey, Doubles…), how you name strength (field/boundary, TE side, receiver side), gaps and techniques, anything you'd never say that the app currently says.
23. The "Teach CounterScheme" box: would you rather talk (voice) or type? Give us five sentences you'd actually say to it.
24. When it isn't sure what you meant, should it ask you right away, or save a list of questions for later?
25. Should every rule it files wait for your confirm before it counts, or do you want it to trust you and let you fix mistakes after?
26. Your philosophy: give us the three-word headline and the paragraph. (Currently "Stop the run. Take away the big play.")
27. Situational packages: which ones exist for you — 3rd down, red zone, goal line, 2-minute, 4-minute, backed up, prevent, 2-point?
28. Diagrams: do you need drawings in this first version? For what — install, practice cards, wristbands? Or are written rules enough for now?

## D. Defensive Analysis

29. When you check your own defense for holes, what do you actually look at? Walk us through how you'd stress-test it yourself.
30. Which offensive looks should it *always* check your defense against? Which ones have burned you?
31. What's the difference, to you, between a "concern" and a "recommendation"? How blunt should it be?
32. Should it ever suggest something you *don't* already carry, or only work with your calls? (Your spec said only what you carry — confirming.)
33. Should it re-run automatically every time you change something, or only when you press a button?

## E. Opponent Matchup

34. Send us a real Hudl breakdown/tendency export — play-by-play, even from last season. Which columns do you tag (down, distance, hash, formation, personnel, motion, play type, play name, result)?
35. Which tendencies matter most to you: run/pass by down & distance, formation usage, hash, field zone, personnel, motion, tempo, red zone, 3rd-down conversions, chunk plays?
36. Down & distance buckets: is Short = 1–3, Medium = 4–6, Long = 7+ how you think about it?
37. Do you talk in personnel numbers ("11", "12", "21") or in formation names? Both?
38. Key players: what do you want recorded — number, position, size, class, what they do, how you plan to handle them?
39. Do you scout their coaching staff, tempo, and style like the mock shows? How much of that do you actually know before a game?
40. The Plan Status checklist — Scouting Report → Tendencies → Game Plan → Walkthrough → Practice Plan. Right steps, right order? What does "done" mean for each?
41. "Ask CounterScheme": give us ten real questions you'd ask about an opponent.

## F. Game Plan

42. What does your current game plan look like physically? Send one from last season. Is it a call sheet with situations down the side?
43. The five outputs — Top Threats, Best Answers We Already Have, Concerns, Small Adjustments, Practice/Call Emphasis. Right buckets? Anything missing (personnel matchups, opening calls, special situations)?
44. "3 to 5 answers" — per bucket, or total? How long should the whole plan be? Would you print it? Put any of it on a wristband?
45. Should it propose actual calls by down and distance (a call sheet), or stay at the "priorities" level?
46. Practice: should it suggest practice periods or drills? Do you script practice, and would you want that in the app?
47. When should a plan be generated — automatically once the opponent is entered, or only when you press the button? If you regenerate, is it OK that it overwrites your edits, or do you want to keep versions?

## G. The AI part (plain English)

48. For the app to use ChatGPT's brain, we need something called an *API account* from OpenAI. It's separate from the ChatGPT app you pay for — it's a pay-per-use account for software, and at your usage it's a few dollars a month. Are you OK creating one (we'll walk you through it) and having it billed to you, so the product is truly yours? Or would you rather Matt set it up under his name for now?
49. How much should the AI talk — short answers, or explain its reasoning? What tone — coach-to-coach, blunt?
50. What must it *never* do? (Invent plays you don't run, change your calls, use words you don't use…)
51. Are you comfortable with player names and info being sent to the AI company when it thinks? We can use jersey numbers only if you'd rather.

## H. What we cut, and what's left

52. We removed the Calendar, Practice Planner, the standalone Ask AI page, and the This Week page, and hid the play-drawing tool. Will you miss any of them right away?
53. Reports: what would a season report need to show you?
54. Navigation: the top bar and the left sidebar — keep both, or one?
55. Do you need this on more than one device, or shared with assistants, *this* season? (That decides when we build accounts and sync.)

## I. What winning looks like
